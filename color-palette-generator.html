Guide

How to choose brand colors that work

A good brand palette isn't just five colors that look nice together — each color needs a job. Use this simple structure:

  • Primary color — the color people should remember. Use it for your logo and key moments.
  • Secondary color — supports the primary in graphics, illustrations and backgrounds.
  • Accent color — a bright color for buttons, links and "Buy now" moments. Use it sparingly.
  • Dark neutral — for text. Near-black or a very dark version of your primary reads well.
  • Light neutral — for backgrounds and cards. Off-whites feel softer than pure white.

Color harmonies explained

HarmonyWhat it isBest for
AnalogousNeighboring hues on the color wheelCalm, natural brands — wellness, food, interiors
ComplementaryOpposite huesHigh-energy brands and ads that need to stand out
TriadicThree evenly spaced huesPlayful brands, kids' products, events
Split complementaryA hue plus the two neighbors of its oppositeVibrant but balanced palettes — easier than complementary
TetradicFour hues in two complementary pairsRich illustration and campaign work
MonochromeOne hue in many shadesMinimal, premium and tech brands

Check readability before you commit

Text needs enough contrast with its background — a ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text (WCAG AA). Paste any color into the color converter to check it against white and black text.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the color palette generator work?

Each click picks a base hue and builds the other colors from a color-harmony rule (analogous, complementary, triadic, split complementary, tetradic or monochrome). Lightness is spread from dark to light so every palette has usable background, text and accent colors.

Can I build a palette around my brand color?

Yes. Type your brand color into the Start from a color box (HEX, RGB, HSL or a color name) and press Use color. Your exact color stays in the palette and the rest are generated to match it.

How do I keep a color I like?

Click the lock icon on a swatch. Locked colors stay put when you generate again, so you can build a palette one color at a time.

How do I save or share a palette?

The page address updates with your colors, so copy the share link to come back later or send it to a client. You can also download a PNG image, or copy the codes as CSS variables, Tailwind config, HEX, RGB or JSON.

How many colors should a brand palette have?

Most brands work well with 3–5 colors: one or two main brand colors, a dark color for text, a light color for backgrounds and one accent for buttons and highlights. Use the 5-color setting as a starting point.

Is the palette generator free for commercial use?

Yes. It's completely free, needs no sign-up, and the colors you create are yours to use in logos, websites, packaging and ads. Colors themselves can't be owned, so there's nothing to credit.

Is there a keyboard shortcut?

Yes. Press the Space bar to generate a new palette (when you are not typing in a box).
