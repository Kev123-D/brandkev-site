FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert HEX to RGB?

Paste the HEX code (for example #003FFC) into the box and the RGB value appears instantly — here rgb(0, 63, 252). Manually, split the six digits into three pairs and convert each pair from base 16 to a number from 0 to 255.

What color formats can I enter?

HEX (#003FFC or 03F), RGB like rgb(0, 63, 252) or just 0, 63, 252, HSL like hsl(225, 100%, 49%), CMYK like cmyk(100, 75, 0, 1), and any of the 148 CSS color names such as coral or navy.

What's the difference between HEX, RGB and HSL?

They describe the same screen color in different ways. HEX is a compact code used in web and design tools. RGB gives the red, green and blue light values (0–255). HSL describes hue, saturation and lightness, which makes it easy to create lighter or darker versions of a color.

Is the CMYK conversion print-accurate?

It's a standard mathematical conversion and a good starting point. Real print results depend on the paper, ink and your printer's color profile, so always ask your printer for a proof. See the CMYK & Pantone guide for tips.

What does the contrast check mean?

It shows the WCAG contrast ratio of white and black text on your color. 4.5:1 or higher passes AA for normal text; 3:1 passes only for large text (about 24px+). Higher is easier to read.

What are tints and shades?

Tints are lighter versions of a color (mixed toward white) and shades are darker versions (mixed toward black). The row of nine swatches keeps your hue and saturation and steps the lightness from 90% down to 10% — handy for hover states, backgrounds and borders.
