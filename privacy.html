Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: we only collect the details you choose to give us when you book a call, email us or message us on WhatsApp. We use them to reply to you and provide our services. We don't sell your information, and our website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

1. Who we are

This website, brandkev.com, is operated by BrandKev ("we", "us", "our"), a digital marketing and brand creative business based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website or contact us.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • When you book a call: your name, email address, and any details you add in the booking form, such as your phone or WhatsApp number, business name and what you need help with.
  • When you email us at hello@brandkev.com: your email address, name and the contents of your message.
  • When you message us on WhatsApp: your phone number, WhatsApp profile name and your messages.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: like almost every website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you request. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix problems.
  • Website analytics: we may use privacy-friendly analytics that count visits and show which pages are popular. These do not use cookies and do not identify you personally.

Our website itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party services you choose to use from our site, such as our booking page or WhatsApp, may use their own cookies under their own policies.

3. How we use your information

  • To arrange and hold your introductory call, and to send reminders about it
  • To reply to your questions and send you quotes or proposals you ask for
  • To provide our services if you become a client, and to manage invoices and records
  • To run, secure and improve our website
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations

We will not add you to a marketing mailing list unless you have agreed to it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

4. Services we use

We use trusted third-party providers to run our website and business. They only process your information as needed to provide their service:

  • Vercel: hosts our website
  • Cal.com: handles call bookings, with the booking added to our calendar
  • Google: calendar and video meetings, and Google Fonts, which loads the typeface used on this site
  • Zoho Mail: our email service for hello@brandkev.com
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you choose to message us there

Some of these providers store data on servers outside the UAE. Where that happens, we rely on providers that apply recognised security and privacy safeguards.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with the service providers listed above, with professional advisers such as our accountant where needed, or when required by law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking details for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and generally no longer than 24 months if you don't become a client. Client records are kept for as long as our relationship continues and then for the period required by UAE law for business and tax records. After that, we delete or anonymise them.

7. How we protect it

Our website uses a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS). We limit access to your information to people who need it to work with you, and we use reputable providers with strong security. No method of storage or transmission online is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) and other laws that may apply to you, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete your information, where we don't need to keep it for legal reasons
  • Stop or limit how we use it, including for any marketing

To make a request, email us at hello@brandkev.com. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
